# ExpensePDF Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

ExpensePDF is an iPhone app made by Best360 Ltd ("we", "us"). It turns photos of paper receipts into clearly named PDF files. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

- ExpensePDF doesn't collect any of your data. There are no accounts, servers, analytics, adverts or tracking.
- Your receipts are read and stored only on your iPhone.
- They leave your iPhone only when you choose to share them.

## Your receipts

For each receipt you scan, ExpensePDF keeps these on your iPhone:

- the photo of the receipt;
- a black-and-white PDF of it;
- the date, company name and type of expense used to name the PDF.

They're stored inside the app on your iPhone, and are included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you use them. We can't see or access them.

## How receipts are read

ExpensePDF reads the date, company and type of expense on your iPhone itself. It uses Apple's built-in text recognition, the iPhone's spelling dictionary and, on iPhones that have it, Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to any other service to do this, and it works without an internet connection.

## Camera

ExpensePDF uses the camera only while you're scanning a receipt. The photos are kept only as described above.

## Sharing

When you share a PDF, you choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, Files, Mail or Messages) using Apple's share sheet. The app or service you choose receives the file, and its own privacy policy applies.

## Purchases

ExpensePDF Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app checks your subscription with Apple on your iPhone.

To limit the free plan to 10 scans, the app keeps the number of free scans you've used in your iPhone's keychain. It's just a number. It's synced to your other Apple devices if you use iCloud Keychain, and it stays if you delete the app, so the free scans aren't reset.

## Information Apple may share with us

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us that don't identify you. Apple also gives us sales reports. Apple's privacy policy covers this.

## Deleting your data

- To delete one receipt, swipe left on it in the list, or open it and tap Delete.
- To delete every receipt, go to Settings, then Delete All Receipts.
- Deleting the app removes all its receipts from your iPhone. Copies in your backups are removed as those backups are replaced. PDFs you've already shared stay wherever you shared them.

## Children

ExpensePDF is a tool for business expenses and isn't meant for children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
