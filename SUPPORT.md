# ExpensePDF Support

Need help with ExpensePDF? Email **[hello@best360.co.uk](mailto:hello@best360.co.uk)** and tell us your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Common questions

### What do I need to use ExpensePDF?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Apple Intelligence isn't needed. Where it's turned on, ExpensePDF uses it as a second opinion when reading receipts.

### How do I scan a receipt?

1. Tap **Scan Receipt**.
2. Hold your iPhone over the receipt. The scanner takes the photo when it finds the receipt's edges, or you can tap the shutter button.
3. Check the photo, then tap **Use Photo**, or **Retake** to try again.
4. Check the details, then tap **Save**.

For the best results, lay the receipt flat on a darker surface in good light.

### Why are some details highlighted in orange?

ExpensePDF wasn't sure it read them correctly, for example when a word on the receipt was hard to read. Check them and correct them if needed before you save. If you change the type of expense for a company, ExpensePDF remembers it for that company's next receipt.

### How are the PDFs named?

By date, type of expense and company, like `171026_Meals_HarbourStreetCafe.pdf` for a meal at Harbour Street Cafe on 17 October 2026. If two receipts would have the same name, the second gets `_2` at the end. Changing a receipt's details renames its PDF.

### How do I save receipts to Google Drive, Files or email?

Open a receipt and tap **Share**. To share several at once, tap **Select** in the list, tick the receipts and tap **Share**. Then choose Google Drive (you'll need the Google Drive app), **Save to Files**, Mail or anywhere else in the list.

### How many free scans do I get?

10 in total. After that, ExpensePDF Pro gives you unlimited scans: either Pro Yearly, which renews every year, or Pro Lifetime, which you pay for once. Your receipts stay available to view, edit and share either way.

### I've paid for Pro but the app doesn't show it

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, then go to **Settings** and tap **Restore Purchases**.

### How do I cancel Pro Yearly?

In ExpensePDF, go to **Settings** and tap **Manage Subscription**. You can also cancel in the iPhone Settings app: tap your name, then **Subscriptions**. Pro carries on until the end of the year you've paid for. Pro Lifetime never renews, so there's nothing to cancel.

### I bought Lifetime but I'm still subscribed to Pro Yearly

Lifetime covers everything, so cancel Pro Yearly in **Settings** → **Manage Subscription** to stop it renewing.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Where are my receipts kept? Will they move to a new iPhone?

Only on your iPhone. They're included in your iPhone's backups, so they come with you when you set up a new iPhone from a backup. To keep copies elsewhere, share the PDFs to Google Drive or Files.

### Does ExpensePDF need the internet?

No. Scanning and reading receipts happen on your iPhone. You only need a connection to buy or restore Pro, and to share to online services.

### How do I delete my data?

Swipe left on a receipt to delete it, or go to **Settings** and tap **Delete All Receipts** to delete them all. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.
